Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Medical Bayes (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to perform Bayesian probability calculations. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the App.

The App does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address.

2. Calculation Data

The App may store your recent calculation results locally on your device so that you can view your calculation history.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

3. Internet Access

The App does not require an internet connection for its core calculation functionality.

If the App contains links to external websites, those websites are governed by their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services to collect or process personal information.

If third-party services are added to the App in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

6. Data Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal information stored on our servers that could be compromised.

Any calculation history stored by the App is stored locally on your device and is subject to the security of your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
